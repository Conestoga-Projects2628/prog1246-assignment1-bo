A Franchise That Defined a Generation

Since its debut in 2003, Call of Duty has grown from a modest World War II shooter into a global cultural phenomenon. Developed primarily by Infinity Ward, Treyarch, and Sledgehammer Games, the franchise has sold over 400 million copies worldwide, making it one of the best-selling video game series in history.

From the gritty realism of the original campaign to the adrenaline-pumping battle royale of Warzone, Call of Duty continues to push the boundaries of what a first-person shooter can be. Each annual release brings new weapons, maps, operators, and storylines that keep millions of players locked in.

Whether you're storming the beaches of Normandy, dropping into Verdansk with your squad, or climbing the ranks in competitive multiplayer, Call of Duty delivers an experience that is as intense as it is unforgettable.
